A note from the unit

We send out a short email most Fridays at sunrise. It's literally typed up at the desk before we go for breakfast. Written by whoever's still awake.

It tells you what came in this week, what's going out next week, who's on holiday, and occasionally why Bay 2 still smells of damp. No images, no tracking pixels, no pop-ups, no offers. Plain text.

About 4,200 people get it. Most of them have never been to the unit. That's fine.
